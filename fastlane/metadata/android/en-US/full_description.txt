Bongo Budget is a sleek, offline-first personal finance and category quota manager for Android. Designed around the Bangladeshi Taka (৳) and built with a pure AMOLED deep black theme, it brings discipline and clarity to your finances.

Features:
- Master Overall Monthly Budget with live shrinking available pool
- Daily Spendable Allowance calculations with one-touch toggle
- Strict Category Quotas to prevent unbudgeted expense leaks
- Custom Categories with dynamic keyword-based icons and colors
- Category Drill-down bottom sheet to inspect individual category expenses
- 100% Offline-First: SQLite Room database on your device with complete privacy
- Dual-mode JSON backup and restore (Merge or Replace)
